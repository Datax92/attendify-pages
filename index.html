Attendify is designed for businesses and organizations that require a reliable employee attendance management system. The application simplifies attendance tracking while improving transparency, productivity, and workforce management.

The application supports secure authentication, GPS location verification, attendance history, employee profile management, real-time monitoring, and cloud synchronization.
